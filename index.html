ABOUT SHERPAS OF SOUTH INDIA

Ride Together. Explore Together.

A community of passionate riders united by a love for motorcycles, exploration and brotherhood. From scenic coastal roads to winding mountain trails, we ride together, discover new destinations and create lasting memories — always promoting responsible and safe riding.

🏔 Adventure Touring
🤝 Brotherhood
🛡 Responsible Riding

Motorcycle Riding Club in South India

Sherpas of South India brings riders together through group rides, touring and a shared passion for motorcycles, exploration and brotherhood.

We ride across Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka—from weekend escapes to longer adventures—while promoting safe riding, mutual support and lasting friendships.
