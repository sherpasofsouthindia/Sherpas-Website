ABOUT SHERPAS OF SOUTH INDIA

Ride Together. Explore Together.

Sherpas of South India is a motorcycle riding club and riding community based in Kerala, bringing together passionate motorcycle enthusiasts from across South India. We organize group rides, motorcycle tours, adventure journeys, social events and community activities while promoting responsible and safe riding. Our members share a passion for exploring new destinations, building lasting friendships and experiencing the open road together.

Motorcycle Riding Club in Kerala

Sherpas of South India is a motorcycle riding club and riding community based in Kerala, bringing together riders who share a passion for motorcycles, touring and exploring South India. Our community organizes group motorcycle rides, touring expeditions, adventure journeys, social gatherings and rider-focused activities.

Our motorcycle rides cover destinations across Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, giving members opportunities to explore different landscapes, roads and destinations while enjoying the experience of riding together. From weekend rides to longer motorcycle touring journeys, Sherpas of South India provides a platform for riders to connect, explore and build lasting friendships through responsible and safe riding.

Motorcycle Rides & Touring Across South India

We bring together motorcycle enthusiasts for group rides, touring, adventure journeys and community activities across South India. Sherpas of South India encourages responsible riding, mutual support among riders and a strong sense of motorcycle community.
